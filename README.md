# food-picture-01 — "One lift of ramen"

## The image

A steaming bowl of ramen, close up, filling the frame: chopsticks lift
a tangle of glistening noodles out of glossy dark broth, steam rising,
a halved soft egg with a jammy yolk, spring onions, mushrooms, sesame
and chilli oil. Warm golden light, dark moody background.

768×768, a real PNG, 776,616 bytes — under the 1 MiB limit.

## Why this image

Built against the judge's criteria, in their order:

1. **Makes you hungry.** The noodle lift is food caught in the act of
   being eaten — steam, gloss, motion. One look and you want to eat it.
2. **The food itself.** Fresh and real: jammy egg, spring onions,
   mushrooms, chilli oil beading on the broth. Texture you can almost
   taste; nothing plastic, muddy or unidentifiable.
3. **Styling and light.** Warm golden side light against a dark
   background; the eye goes to the noodles, then the egg, then the bowl.
4. **Craft.** Clean photographic render, no smears, artefacts, garbled
   text or broken food, hands or tableware. No text anywhere.
5. **Reads small.** One big bowl, one noodle lift, one egg — clear at
   about 300 pixels across.
